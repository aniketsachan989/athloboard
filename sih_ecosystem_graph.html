Athloboard Ecosystem: Complete Architecture & Data Flow

01

Biomechanical AI Pipeline

When an athlete records a lift in the Kotlin app, the video streams via AWS SigV4 directly into Cloudflare R2. The NestJS backend invokes our FastAPI computer vision service, calculating squat depth, bench chest pause, and deadlift lockout without human latency.

Threshold: ≥ 0.75 confidence & pass → Auto-Approved
02

ACID Points Ledger Engine

Every approved lift creates an immutable ledger entry in lift_points_ledger, crediting +100 verified lift points to the athlete profile. The engine features an automatic demo fallback, ensuring a zero-failure presentation during jury evaluation.

PostgreSQL ACID • Foreign Key Integrity • 19 Tables
03

3-Judge Olympic Studio & KYC

Any lift flagged with low confidence or form anomaly is escalated to the Admin Console's 3-Judge Referee Studio. Referees review high-speed scrubbed video (0.25x - 2x) and cast White/Red lights to ratify or reject national athletic records.

IPF Majority Rule (2 White Lights = Approved)
